Trigonometry

Vocabulary

  • Hypotenuse

    • The longest side of a right triangle, opposite the right angle.

  • Opposite

    • The side opposite to the given angle in a right triangle.

  • Adjacent

    • The side next to the given angle in a right triangle.

  • Angle of Incline

    • The angle that rises up from the horizontal.

  • Angle of Elevation

    • The angle formed by the line of sight and the horizontal when looking up at an object.

  • Angle of Depression

    • The angle formed by the line of sight and the horizontal when looking down at an object.

Formulas

  • SOHCAHTOA

    • Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse

    Cosine = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

    Tangent = Opposite / Adjacent

Tips/Rules

  • If you have the first side of the formula and are trying to find the second side then divide, otherwise multiply.

  • If you don't have the angle, you can use the inverse trigonometric functions to find it.

Examples

  • Example 1

    • Step 1: Identify the necessary formula

    Tangent Formula

    Step 2: Substitute the known values into the formula.

    tan 38.7° = opposite / 100 m

    Step 3: Solve for the unknown value.

    opposite = 100 m * tan 38.7°

    opposite ≈ 79.9 m

  • Example 2

    • Step 1: Identify the necessary formula

    Cosine Formula

    Step 2: Substitute the known values into the formula.

    cos 38.7° = adjacent / 100 m

    Step 3: Solve for the unknown value.

    adjacent = 100 m * cos 38.7°

    adjacent ≈ 78.2 m
