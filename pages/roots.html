Roots & Powers

Vocabulary

  • Base

    • The number that is getting multiplied.

  • Exponent

    • The number of times a base is multiplied by itself.

  • Radical

    • A symbol ( √ ) used to indicate a root.

  • Radicand

    • The number inside the radical symbol.

Formulas

  • Power of a Power

    • (am)n = a(m*n)

  • Power of a Product

    • (ab)n = an * bn

  • Power of a Quotient

    • (a/b)n = an / bn

  • Product of Powers

    • am * an = a(m+n)

  • Quotient of Powers

    • am / an = a(m-n)

  • Negative Exponent

    • a-n = 1 / an

  • Zero Exponent

    • a0 = 1

  • Product of Roots

    • √a * √b = √(ab)

  • Quotient of Roots

    • √a / √b = √(a/b)

  • Power of a Root

    • (√a)n = √(an)

Tips/Rules

  • When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents.

  • When dividing powers with the same base, subtract the exponents.

  • When raising a power to another power, multiply the exponents.

  • When raising a product to a power, raise each factor to the power.

  • When raising a quotient to a power, raise both the numerator and denominator to the power.

  • A negative exponent indicates that the base should be taken as the reciprocal.

  • Any non-zero number raised to the zero power is equal to one.
