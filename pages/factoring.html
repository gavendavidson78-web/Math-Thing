Factoring

Vocabulary

  • Polynomial

    • A mathematical expression consisting of variables and coefficients.

  • Monomial

    • An expression with one term, such as 5x.

  • Binomial

    • An expression with two terms, such as x + 5.

  • Trinomial

    • An expression with three terms, such as x² + 5x + 6.

  • Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

    • The largest factor that two or more numbers have in common.

  • Multivariable

    • An expression with multiple variables, such as 2x + 3y.

  • Factored Form

    • A way of writing a polynomial as a product of its factors.

Formulas

  • FOIL

    • First Terms, Outer Terms, Inside Terms, Last Terms

    (a + b)(c + d) = ac + ad + bc + bd

Tips/Rules

  • If a polynomial has a GCF, factor it out first.

  • If a polynomial is a perfect square trinomial, it can be factored as (a + b)² or (a - b)².

  • If all signs are negative then that means the factors will have only one negative number

Examples

  • Example 1

    • Step 1: Identify the GCF.

    GCF=2

    Step 2: Factor out the GCF.

    2(x2 + 3x - 18)

    Step 3: Factor the remaining polynomial.

    2(x + 6)(x - 3)

  • Example 2

    • Step 1: Identify the GCF.

    GCF=1

    Step 2: Factor out the GCF.

    (x2 - 6x + 8)

    Step 3: Factor the remaining polynomial.

    (x - 4)(x - 2)
